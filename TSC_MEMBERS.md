## Security contacts

Vulnerability reports are received through the channels documented in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and are triaged by the TSC and its designees. Do not send vulnerability reports to individual members.

## Code of Conduct responders

Code of Conduct concerns are received at <ibc@interchain.io> and handled by the TSC Chair together with the Maintainer seats listed above. If a report concerns one of them, escalate using the process in the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Maintainers and Committers

The Maintainers and Committers who hold merge rights on this repository, and the process for joining and leaving that group, are in [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md). Code ownership is enforced through [CODEOWNERS](./.github/CODEOWNERS).
